# User Manual

## Login
Use password, PIN or biometric login.

Default:
- Password: admin123
- PIN: 1234

## Billing
1. Tap Start Billing.
2. Enter buyer and consignee details.
3. Add product rows.
4. Select product suggestions to auto-fill HSN, GST, rate and unit.
5. Check totals.
6. Save invoice.
7. Generate PDF, preview/print, share or upload to Google Drive.

## Product Master
Add products with HSN, rate, GST %, unit and category.

## Customer Master
Save customers for auto-fill during billing.

## Reports
View daily, weekly, monthly and yearly reports. Export CSV for Excel.

## Backup
Settings → Manual Backup.
